## AGENTS.md is a map, not a manual

<!-- key: agents-md-map -->

A row earns its place by saying something `ls` does not, and a section earns its place by saying
something a skill's own `SKILL.md` does not already say better. Point at the source of truth — a package's
own README, an ADR, a template — rather than restating it; the restatement is the copy that goes stale.
